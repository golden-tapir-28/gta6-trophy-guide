    Where the facts come from

    The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as reported by Push Square via Exophase and matched by GTABase, PowerPyx, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer. Each trophy’s guide cites its own sources beside it — the counts, locations and conditions in a guide come from the sources named there, and the words are ours.
